# Business Expense Policy — Meridian Software Inc.

## Purpose

Meridian Software Inc. reimburses reasonable, necessary, and properly documented business expenses incurred by employees in the course of company work. This policy establishes clear spending limits, approval thresholds, submission deadlines, and reimbursement timing so that employees, managers, and Finance can apply rules consistently. The policy is designed to be auditable: every major control includes a specific dollar amount, day count, or named approver role.

Employees are expected to spend company resources as carefully as they would spend their own money on a necessary business purpose, to choose cost-effective options, and to avoid expenses that are personal in nature. This policy applies to travel, meals, lodging, ground transportation, mileage, and other ordinary business expenses submitted through the company’s expense process. Pacific Time is the headquarters timezone for interpreting calendar-day deadlines unless Finance communicates a different cutoff for a specific payroll cycle.

Questions may be directed to Finance using the appropriate @meridian-software.example contact published in the expense tool. The VP of Finance owns this policy.

## Scope and Eligibility

This policy applies to all employees of Meridian Software Inc. who incur business expenses. Contractors are reimbursed only as stated in their agreements and are not automatically entitled to the stipends or per-diems in this employee policy. Expenses must be for a legitimate Meridian Software Inc. business purpose, incurred while performing job duties, and compliant with the limits below.

Personal expenses, commuting between home and a regular office location on ordinary workdays, and luxury upgrades that exceed policy caps without approval are not reimbursable. When a trip mixes business and personal time, only the business-required portion is reimbursable, and airfare or lodging must be allocated accordingly with a clear explanation in the expense report.

## Meal Per-Diem Limits

For business travel involving overnight stays or authorized out-of-area workdays, Meridian Software Inc. uses meal per-diem limits rather than encouraging extravagant dining. The domestic meal per-diem is $75 per day. The international meal per-diem is $110 per day. Employees should not treat the per-diem as a target to maximize; it is a ceiling for reimbursable meal costs for the day unless a documented exception is approved under the approval thresholds in this policy.

The per-diem covers breakfast, lunch, dinner, and incidental soft drinks or similar items associated with meals. It does not increase automatically because multiple employees dine together; each traveler remains subject to their own daily limit based on receipts or the company’s configured per-diem method in the expense system. Alcohol is addressed separately and is not included as a routine part of the meal per-diem.

On partial travel days, employees should claim a reasonable amount that reflects actual need and remains within the applicable $75 domestic or $110 international daily ceiling. Lavish group dinners that push each attendee above the daily limit are not made reimbursable simply because a customer attended; the approval hierarchy must be used if a higher amount is truly necessary.

## Lodging Caps

Domestic hotel lodging is capped at $250 per night. International hotel lodging is capped at $350 per night. Employees should select standard business-class hotels that are safe, reasonably close to the work location, and within cap. Resort fees and mandatory taxes may be reimbursed when customary and documented; optional upgrades, spa packages, and mini-bar personal items are not reimbursable.

If lodging within the cap is unavailable due to conferences, city-wide demand, or customer-required hotels, the employee must obtain approval from the appropriate approver under the dollar thresholds in this policy before booking when feasible, or as soon as practicable thereafter, and must attach evidence of limited availability. Booking luxury suites or unnecessary additional rooms for personal comfort is prohibited.

Cancellation fees may be reimbursable when travel is changed for valid business reasons and the employee made reasonable efforts to avoid fees. Fees caused by personal preference changes are the employee’s responsibility.

## Ground Transportation

Employees should use cost-effective ground transportation such as rideshare, taxi, public transit, or hotel shuttles. Receipts are required for rideshare or taxi charges over $25. Amounts of $25 or less should still be recorded accurately; Finance may request receipts even below that threshold when patterns warrant review.

Airport transfers, travel between customer sites, and travel between the hotel and the business location are generally reimbursable when they are ordinary and necessary. Side trips for sightseeing, visits to friends, or personal errands are not reimbursable. Split rides that mix personal and business purposes must allocate only the business portion to Meridian Software Inc.

Rental cars require manager approval in advance and should be used when cost-effective compared with rideshare for the itinerary. Employees must decline unnecessary insurance products when corporate coverage already applies as determined by Finance guidance, and must not rent exotic or luxury vehicles.

## Mileage Reimbursement

When an employee uses a personal vehicle for approved business travel, Meridian Software Inc. reimburses mileage at $0.67 per mile. Mileage is measured using a reasonable route between business locations or between home and a temporary business location when such travel is otherwise reimbursable under company practice and applicable law. Ordinary commuting miles to the employee’s regular office are not reimbursable.

Employees must document the date, business purpose, starting point, ending point, and miles driven. Fuel, routine maintenance, and insurance for personal vehicles are not separately reimbursed because they are considered addressed through the $0.67 per mile rate. Parking and tolls necessary to the business trip may be reimbursed with receipts when required by the expense system.

## Alcohol

Alcohol is not reimbursable, with one narrow exception: a single drink purchased with a client dinner may be reimbursed up to $20 with an itemized receipt. The client dinner must be a bona fide business meal, the drink must appear on an itemized receipt, and the expense report must identify the client and business purpose. Bar tabs, rounds of drinks, alcohol without a meal, alcohol above $20 for the single allowed drink, and alcohol at internal-only team dinners are not reimbursable under this exception.

Managers must not pressure employees to purchase alcohol, and employees must follow all applicable laws regarding age and impairment. Safety comes first; impaired driving is strictly prohibited.

## Payment Methods: Personal and Corporate Cards

Meridian Software Inc. prefers that employees use personal cards for ordinary business expenses and then submit for reimbursement under this policy. Corporate cards are issued for employees at Director level and above. Corporate cardholders must still comply with per-diems, lodging caps, alcohol rules, receipt requirements, and approval thresholds. A corporate card does not authorize spending beyond policy.

Corporate cards are company property and must not be used for personal purchases. Accidental personal charges must be reported and repaid promptly according to Finance instructions. Loss of a corporate card must be reported immediately to Finance and, if fraud is suspected, also aligned with security reporting expectations.

Employees below Director level should not request corporate cards except where Finance creates a documented program exception for a specific role with high booking volume. Even then, personal-card reimbursement remains available and preferred when practical.

## Approval Authority

Expense approval authority is based on the total amount of the expense report or the expense item as configured in the company’s workflow, and is as follows:

- Manager approval is required for expenses under $500.
- Director approval is required for expenses of $500 through $2,500.
- VP of Finance approval is required for expenses above $2,500.

Employees must not split a single logical purchase into multiple submissions to evade thresholds. Managers and Directors must review business purpose, policy caps (including $75/$110 meals and $250/$350 lodging), receipt quality, and alcohol compliance before approving. Approvers who routinely approve out-of-policy spend without documented justification may lose approval rights and may face corrective action.

When the expense is incurred by a Director, the approver must be the next higher authority appropriate to the amount, and expenses above $2,500 still require VP of Finance approval. Self-approval is prohibited.

## Submission Deadlines and Reimbursement Timing

Employees must submit expense reports within 14 calendar days of the expense date. Late submissions impede accruals and audits. Habitual lateness may result in delayed reimbursement or progressive coaching. If an employee is traveling continuously, interim submissions should be made so that no single expense ages beyond 14 calendar days from its expense date without submission.

After an expense report is submitted and approved, reimbursement is paid within 30 calendar days of approved submission. Employees should ensure banking details in the HRIS or payment system are current. Finance is not responsible for delays caused by incorrect account information supplied by the employee.

Missing receipts, missing business purpose, or missing required approvals reset the process; the 30 calendar day reimbursement timing runs from approved submission, not from the original incomplete attempt. Employees should monitor their inbox at their @meridian-software.example address for workflow notifications.

## Receipts and Documentation

Itemized receipts are required as specified by the expense system and are always required for the alcohol exception up to $20, for rideshare or taxi charges over $25, and for lodging and airfare. Credit card totals without detail are often insufficient when the purchase could include non-reimbursable items. Soft copies are acceptable if legible.

Each expense line must state a clear business purpose. Customer names, meeting topics, or internal project codes should be included when relevant. For mileage at $0.67 per mile, route details must be sufficient for Finance to validate reasonableness.

Foreign currency expenses should be submitted with the currency conversion method used by the expense tool or with supporting documentation. Employees should not invent favorable exchange rates.

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses

Examples of non-reimbursable expenses include personal entertainment, gifts that are not part of an approved corporate gift program, childcare or pet care during travel unless a legally required accommodation is approved through People Operations, clothing (except required safety gear approved in advance), traffic fines, and airline club memberships unless pre-approved under the approval thresholds as a documented business need. Expenses that exceed meal per-diems of $75 domestic or $110 international per day, or lodging caps of $250 domestic or $350 international per night, are not reimbursable to the extent of the overage unless properly approved as an exception through the Manager / Director / VP of Finance authority ladder.

## Travel Booking Practices

Employees should book travel with reasonable advance notice when possible to control airfare and lodging costs. Preferred airlines or hotels may be used when they remain within policy and serve the business itinerary. Companion travel for personal guests is not reimbursable, and any room differential caused by a personal guest is the employee’s responsibility.

If a meeting is virtual-capable and travel is not necessary, managers should decline travel requests that create avoidable spend. Sustainability and cost discipline both support choosing remote participation when outcomes will not suffer.

## Manager and Finance Responsibilities

Managers must verify that expenses are necessary, within policy, and correctly coded. Managers must apply the under-$500 approval responsibility carefully and escalate $500–$2,500 items to Directors and amounts above $2,500 to the VP of Finance. Finance audits samples of expense reports, may request additional documentation, and may reverse reimbursement for unallowable items discovered after payment.

People Operations may be consulted when an expense issue overlaps with accommodations, leave, or workplace concerns. Security should be consulted when an expense report suggests data handling risk (for example, unapproved USB purchases for storing Confidential data), without delaying the core Finance workflow for ordinary compliant expenses.

## Policy Ownership

The VP of Finance owns this policy and approves material changes to caps, thresholds, and reimbursement timelines. Finance will publish procedural job aids for the expense tool as needed. In a conflict between a job aid and this policy, this policy controls unless the VP of Finance issues a written update.

Employees are expected to know and apply the domestic meal per-diem of $75 per day, the international meal per-diem of $110 per day, the domestic hotel cap of $250 per night, the international hotel cap of $350 per night, the rideshare/taxi receipt requirement over $25, the mileage rate of $0.67 per mile, the alcohol exception of one client-dinner drink up to $20 with itemized receipt, the approval thresholds (Manager under $500; Director $500–$2,500; VP of Finance above $2,500), the requirement to submit within 14 calendar days of the expense date, and reimbursement within 30 calendar days of approved submission.

## Compliance and Misuse

Falsifying expense reports, fabricating receipts, claiming personal trips as business travel, or intentionally exceeding limits without approval constitutes serious misconduct and may result in corrective action up to and including termination, repayment demands, and legal remedies. Good-faith errors should be reported to Finance as soon as discovered for correction.

Meridian Software Inc. values trust. Most employees will find that following these specific, testable rules makes reimbursement faster and reduces back-and-forth with approvers. When a customer situation requires an exception, use the approval ladder early rather than seeking forgiveness after an out-of-policy booking.
