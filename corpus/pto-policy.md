# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy — Meridian Software Inc.

## Purpose

Meridian Software Inc. provides Paid Time Off (PTO) so employees can rest, manage personal obligations, and maintain a sustainable balance between work and life. This policy explains how PTO is earned, requested, approved, carried over, and administered for employees of Meridian Software Inc. It also describes the separate sick leave bank, unpaid leave of absence options, blackout periods that apply to certain teams, and the responsibilities of managers and People Operations.

The company expects employees to plan time away thoughtfully, communicate early with their managers, and respect coverage needs for customers and internal stakeholders. At the same time, managers are expected to support reasonable use of accrued PTO and to avoid practices that discourage employees from taking earned time off. This policy applies company-wide and is administered under Pacific Time for accrual cutoffs, request deadlines, and calendar-year boundaries unless a local law requires otherwise.

Questions about this policy may be directed to People Operations at people@meridian-software.example. Escalations related to exceptions or complex leave situations should be routed through the employee’s manager and People Operations.

## Eligibility

All regular full-time and regular part-time employees of Meridian Software Inc. are eligible to accrue PTO under this policy, subject to the accrual schedules and pro-ration rules described below. Temporary workers, interns engaged under short-term agreements that do not include benefits, and independent contractors are not eligible for PTO accrual unless a written agreement expressly states otherwise.

New hires begin accruing PTO on their first day of employment in an eligible classification. PTO may be requested and used during the employee’s first 90 calendar days of employment (the probation period), provided the employee has accrued sufficient hours or days to cover the absence and the manager approves the request. Using PTO during probation does not extend or shorten the 90 calendar day probation period.

Employees who transfer from an ineligible classification into an eligible classification begin accruing on the effective date of the transfer. Employees who move from part-time to full-time, or the reverse, will have their accrual rate adjusted prospectively based on the new schedule and, for part-time employees, the applicable FTE percentage.

Eligibility for unpaid leave of absence under this policy requires at least 12 months of continuous service with Meridian Software Inc., as described in the unpaid leave section. Sick leave eligibility is described separately and applies to eligible employees as stated in that section.

## Accrual

PTO for full-time employees accrues on a monthly basis according to years of continuous service with Meridian Software Inc., measured from the employee’s most recent hire date in an eligible role. Accrual rates are as follows:

- Years 1–2: employees accrue 1.25 days per month, which equals 15 days per year.
- Years 3–5: employees accrue 1.67 days per month, which equals 20 days per year.
- Years 6 and beyond: employees accrue 2.08 days per month, which equals 25 days per year.

For purposes of this schedule, an employee enters the Years 3–5 band on the first day of the month following completion of two full years of eligible service, and enters the Years 6+ band on the first day of the month following completion of five full years of eligible service. Accrual rate changes are prospective only; Meridian Software Inc. does not recalculate prior accruals when an employee moves into a higher band.

Part-time employees accrue PTO on a pro-rated basis according to their FTE percentage. For example, a part-time employee at 50% FTE in Years 1–2 accrues one-half of the full-time rate, which is 0.625 days per month (7.5 days per year at a constant 50% FTE). If an employee’s FTE percentage changes mid-year, People Operations will adjust the monthly accrual prospectively to reflect the new FTE percentage. Managers must submit schedule-change paperwork promptly so that accrual remains accurate.

PTO accrues only while an employee is in active paid status. Employees on unpaid leave generally do not accrue PTO during the unpaid portion of the leave, except where applicable law requires continued accrual. PTO does not accrue on unused PTO itself; accrual is based on service and eligibility status, not on the size of the employee’s available balance.

A “day” of PTO for a full-time employee equals the employee’s standard scheduled workday. Employees should review balances in the HRIS and raise discrepancies with people@meridian-software.example promptly.

## Requesting Time Off

Employees must request PTO through the company’s designated time-off system. Informal calendar holds or chat messages are not a substitute for a formal request, although employees are encouraged to discuss plans with their manager before submitting the system request.

Manager approval is required for all PTO. Approval is not automatic; managers evaluate business needs, team coverage, customer commitments, and the employee’s available balance. Employees should not assume approval until the request is marked approved in the system.

For absences of three or more consecutive business days, employees must submit the request at least 10 business days in advance. Requests submitted with less notice may be denied or may require the employee to adjust dates, except where the absence is for an unforeseeable reason that qualifies under sick leave or another protected leave framework. Even for shorter absences, employees are expected to provide as much notice as practicable.

Consecutive absences of more than 15 business days require approval from the VP of People in addition to the employee’s manager. Employees planning extended vacations should initiate discussions early so that coverage plans, customer handoffs, and approval routing can be completed without last-minute disruption. People Operations will coordinate the VP of People review after the manager has indicated support or has identified concerns.

When multiple employees request overlapping time off, managers may approve, deny, or reschedule based on operational needs. Blackout periods may further limit approvals for Engineering and Customer Success during the last 2 weeks of Q4. Employees must hand off critical work before leave begins; unapproved absences may result in corrective action.

## Carryover

Meridian Software Inc. permits employees to carry over a maximum of 5 unused PTO days into the next calendar year. Any unused PTO above 5 days is forfeited at 11:59 PM Pacific Time on December 31 of each calendar year, except where applicable law prohibits forfeiture. Employees are encouraged to monitor balances throughout the year and to schedule time off so that accruals are used rather than lost.

Carried-over days become part of the employee’s available balance on January 1 and may be used in accordance with this policy. Carryover does not increase the employee’s ongoing monthly accrual rate. Accrual continues under the schedule that applies to the employee’s years of service and FTE percentage.

If an employee’s employment ends, payout of unused PTO will be handled in accordance with applicable law and Meridian Software Inc. separation practices. Employees should not rely on year-end forfeiture rules as a substitute for understanding separation payout rules; People Operations can provide guidance at people@meridian-software.example during offboarding.

Managers should not instruct employees to work while on approved PTO as a routine practice. If a genuine emergency requires brief contact, the manager and employee should document the situation and, where appropriate, restore PTO time through People Operations.

## Sick Leave

In addition to PTO, Meridian Software Inc. provides a separate sick leave bank of 8 days per year for eligible employees. Sick leave is intended for the employee’s own illness or injury, preventive care, and other uses required by applicable law, and may also be available to care for a covered family member where required by law or company practice. Sick leave is tracked separately from PTO and may not be used as additional vacation.

Unused sick leave does not carry over from one calendar year to the next. Employees receive a fresh sick leave bank according to company administration practices at the start of each year or upon hire on a pro-rated basis for mid-year starts, as configured in the HRIS. Employees should not treat sick leave as interchangeable with PTO when planning leisure travel.

For planned medical appointments that will consume a full day or multiple days, employees should submit requests in advance when possible. For unforeseeable illness, employees should notify their manager as soon as practicable and submit the corresponding time entry promptly. Meridian Software Inc. may require documentation for absences where permitted by law, particularly for extended absences or patterns that raise attendance concerns.

If an employee exhausts sick leave, the employee may request to use available PTO for continuing absence related to illness, subject to manager approval and applicable law. Use of PTO for illness does not convert that time into sick leave for carryover or other sick-leave-specific rules.

Abuse of sick leave, including falsification of the reason for absence, may result in corrective action up to and including termination of employment, consistent with company policy and applicable law.

## Unpaid Leave of Absence

Employees who have completed at least 12 months of service with Meridian Software Inc. may request an unpaid leave of absence for personal reasons that are not covered by another leave policy or by law. Unpaid leave under this section may be granted for up to 30 calendar days with Director approval. Requests longer than 30 calendar days, or requests that would create significant operational risk, are generally denied under this discretionary program, though the company may consider other arrangements in extraordinary circumstances.

A request for unpaid leave must be submitted in writing to the employee’s manager and People Operations, including the proposed start date, end date, and a general statement of the reason sufficient for the company to evaluate the request. The manager will provide a recommendation; Director approval is required before the leave may begin. Employees should not purchase nonrefundable travel or make irreversible personal commitments until written approval is issued.

During an approved unpaid leave of absence, PTO accrual generally pauses, and benefits continuation will be administered according to the applicable benefits plan documents and People Operations guidance. Employees remain responsible for any required premium contributions during leave. Failure to return on the approved return date without an approved extension may be treated as a voluntary resignation.

Unpaid leave under this section is distinct from legally protected leaves (for example, where family, medical, military, or other statutory leaves apply). When a situation may qualify for protected leave, employees should contact People Operations at people@meridian-software.example promptly so that rights and obligations can be explained and paperwork can be initiated. Nothing in this policy is intended to interfere with rights under applicable law.

## Blackout Periods

To protect year-end delivery, customer support quality, and operational stability, Meridian Software Inc. maintains blackout periods for specific functions. For Engineering and Customer Success, the last 2 weeks of Q4 are a blackout period for PTO unless the absence is pre-approved by a Director.

During a blackout period, managers should not approve routine vacation for covered employees without Director pre-approval. Exceptions may be considered for significant life events, previously approved long-lead plans that a Director has expressly allowed, or situations required by law. Sick leave and protected leave are not “blacked out” by this operational rule; employees who are ill or who are using protected leave should follow the applicable notification and documentation processes.

Directors who pre-approve blackout exceptions must ensure coverage plans are documented, including on-call arrangements, customer escalation paths, and backup ownership for critical tickets or releases. People Operations may audit blackout exceptions periodically to confirm consistent application across teams.

Employees outside Engineering and Customer Success are not automatically subject to the Q4 blackout described above, but their managers may still deny or reschedule PTO based on legitimate business needs, product launches, audits, or customer events. Team-specific busy periods should be communicated as early as possible each year.

## Manager Responsibilities

Managers play a central role in administering this policy fairly and consistently. Managers must:

- Review and decide PTO requests promptly, and ensure that requests for three or more consecutive business days are evaluated against the 10 business day advance-notice expectation.
- Confirm that the employee has a sufficient accrued balance before approving PTO.
- Escalate consecutive absences of more than 15 business days to the VP of People for required additional approval.
- Plan coverage for customer commitments, releases, and internal deadlines before approving overlapping absences.
- Apply blackout rules for Engineering and Customer Success during the last 2 weeks of Q4, including obtaining Director pre-approval for any exception.
- Avoid informal “off the books” arrangements that bypass the time-off system.
- Support employees in using accrued time thoughtfully so that large balances do not accumulate solely due to managerial pressure.
- Consult People Operations when a request may implicate sick leave, unpaid leave, disability, or legally protected leave.

If a manager will be unavailable, the manager must designate an alternate approver with appropriate authority. Favoritism in approving or denying PTO is prohibited. Company-observed holidays are separate from PTO. The HRIS is the system of record for balances; employees should raise discrepancies to people@meridian-software.example promptly.

## Policy Ownership

People Operations owns this policy and is responsible for interpretation, updates, training for managers, and audit of exception patterns. Proposed changes to accrual rates, carryover maximums, sick leave allotments, unpaid leave duration, or blackout rules must be reviewed by People Operations and approved through the company's policy governance process before communication to employees.

Operational contacts:

- General PTO questions: people@meridian-software.example
- Escalations requiring VP of People review for consecutive absences over 15 business days: routed by People Operations after manager review
- Director-level blackout exceptions: requested by the manager and approved by the appropriate Director, with People Operations informed

Meridian Software Inc. may update this policy at any time, with or without prior notice, except where notice is required by law. If any provision conflicts with applicable law, the law controls and the remainder of the policy remains in effect. Employees are expected to follow the accrual schedule (1.25, 1.67, or 2.08 days per month by years of service), the 5-day carryover maximum with December 31 forfeiture of excess, the 8-day sick leave bank with no carryover, the 10 business day advance-notice rule for absences of three or more consecutive business days, VP of People approval for consecutive absences over 15 business days, unpaid leave after 12 months of service for up to 30 calendar days with Director approval, and the Engineering and Customer Success blackout during the last 2 weeks of Q4 unless pre-approved by a Director.
