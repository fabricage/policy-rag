# Meridian Software Inc. Onboarding Policy

## Purpose and Scope

This Onboarding Policy describes how Meridian Software Inc. welcomes new employees, equips them for success, and completes required early-employment steps. The policy applies to all newly hired employees of Meridian Software Inc., including full-time and part-time hires, unless an offer letter or applicable law specifies a different arrangement for a particular step. Contractors are onboarded under separate engagement procedures and are not covered by every employee-specific benefit step in this policy.

Meridian Software Inc. uses Pacific Time as the headquarters timezone for Day-1 meeting deadlines, orientation scheduling, and other time-based onboarding requirements in this policy. People Operations and IT jointly administer onboarding operations, and questions may be sent to people@meridian-software.example or the IT support channel designated during hire.

## Policy Owner

This policy is owned by People Operations jointly with IT. People Operations owns employment paperwork, benefits enrollment coordination, buddy program administration, check-in compliance, and policy interpretation for people processes. IT owns equipment issuance standards, account provisioning, access removal at departure, and security awareness training logistics. Managers own the new hire's role clarity, Day-1 welcome meeting, first-week learning plan, and documented check-ins.

## Probation Period

New employees of Meridian Software Inc. serve a probation period of ninety (90) calendar days from the start date. The probation period is a structured time for mutual evaluation of role fit, performance, collaboration, and conduct. Successful completion of probation does not guarantee continued employment for any specific duration where at-will employment applies; it marks the end of the initial probationary evaluation window described in this policy.

During probation, employment may be ended with one (1) week notice (or pay in lieu) except for misconduct. Misconduct may result in immediate termination without one week notice or pay in lieu, consistent with the Meridian Software Inc. Code of Conduct and applicable law. Managers considering ending employment during probation must consult People Operations before communicating a final decision, except where safety requires immediate removal from the workplace.

## Pre-Start Preparation

Before Day 1, People Operations confirms that the offer is accepted, start date is recorded in the HRIS, and required identity and work-authorization steps are scheduled. IT prepares equipment and account requests based on role. The hiring manager should prepare a first-week plan that includes meaningful work, introductions, and the required sessions listed in this policy.

New hires should receive pre-start communications from an @meridian-software.example address with arrival instructions, dress or workplace norms as applicable, remote setup guidance if relevant, and points of contact. If a start date changes, People Operations and IT must be notified immediately so that equipment and orientation cohort placement can be adjusted.

## Day 1 Equipment and Accounts

On Day 1, Meridian Software Inc. issues the following equipment and access as applicable to the role:

- Laptop (MacBook Pro or Windows laptop per role)  
- Employee badge  
- Headset  
- Accounts (email, Slack, HRIS, source control as applicable)  

IT documents serial numbers and asset tags for issued hardware. Employees must review acceptable-use and security expectations when accounts are activated. If a Day-1 equipment item is unavailable due to supply constraints, IT will provide a documented temporary alternative and a target date for standard issuance. Employees should not purchase substitute equipment expecting reimbursement unless IT and Finance have pre-approved the purchase in writing.

Badge access is granted according to workplace location and role. Lost badges must be reported promptly to IT and People Operations. Source control and other sensitive systems are provisioned on a least-privilege basis; managers must request additional access through approved IT workflows rather than sharing credentials.

## Day 1 Welcome Meeting

The manager must schedule a thirty (30) minute Day-1 welcome meeting before 11:00 AM Pacific. This meeting is a required onboarding milestone, not an optional courtesy. The welcome meeting should cover introductions, role expectations for the first week, communication norms, immediate priorities, and how the new hire can get help. If the manager is unavailable due to emergency, a designated alternate leader must conduct the meeting before 11:00 AM Pacific, and the manager must hold a follow-up meeting within one business day.

People Operations may audit compliance with the Day-1 welcome meeting requirement. Repeated failures by a manager to meet this standard may be addressed through management coaching or performance feedback.

## First-Week Required Schedule

The first-week schedule must include all of the following:

- Security awareness training (due by Day 5)  
- Benefits enrollment session  
- Product overview  
- Buddy introduction  

Managers are responsible for ensuring these items appear on the new hire's calendar and that attendance is possible given role demands. Security awareness training must be completed by Day 5; failure to complete it by Day 5 may result in restricted system access until training is finished. The benefits enrollment session explains available plans and the enrollment deadline. The product overview should give the new hire a working understanding of Meridian Software Inc. offerings and customers at a level appropriate to the role. The buddy introduction launches the onboarding buddy relationship described below.

## Onboarding Buddy

An onboarding buddy is assigned by Day 2. The buddy is a peer resource who helps the new hire navigate tools, culture, and unwritten norms. Buddies are not substitutes for managers and do not conduct formal performance evaluation. People Operations coordinates buddy assignment with the hiring manager, aiming for someone with capacity and sufficient tenure to be helpful.

Buddies should initiate contact by Day 2 if the new hire has not already been introduced, schedule informal check-ins during the first month, and escalate concerns about isolation or missing access to the manager and People Operations. If a buddy becomes unavailable, a replacement buddy should be assigned promptly so that the Day 2 assignment standard remains meaningful in practice.

## Benefits Enrollment Deadline

Benefits enrollment must be completed within thirty (30) calendar days from the start date. Missing this deadline may delay coverage or limit available elections according to plan rules and applicable law. People Operations and benefits administrators provide enrollment instructions during the first-week benefits enrollment session and through HRIS notifications.

Employees with questions about plan selection should seek guidance before the thirtieth calendar day. Life events and special enrollment rights, where applicable, are handled under plan documents and law and may differ from the standard thirty-calendar-day new-hire window. This policy's thirty-calendar-day deadline is the default new-hire enrollment requirement for Meridian Software Inc.

## Check-Ins at 30 Days and 90 Days

Managers must complete thirty (30) day and ninety (90) day check-ins with each new hire and document those check-ins in the HRIS. The 30-day check-in should assess early ramp progress, clarify expectations, identify tooling or training gaps, and confirm that security awareness training, benefits enrollment steps, and buddy engagement occurred. The 90-day check-in aligns with the end of the probation period of ninety calendar days from the start date and should include a clear conversation about performance, conduct, role fit, and goals for the next period.

Documentation in the HRIS must be complete enough for People Operations to confirm that the conversation occurred and to understand any follow-up actions. Managers should not wait until the 90-day check-in to address serious performance or conduct concerns. Early feedback is part of fair onboarding and reduces surprise outcomes during probation.

## New Hire Orientation Cohort

Meridian Software Inc. holds a new hire orientation cohort every other Monday at 9:00 AM Pacific. Orientation introduces company mission, organizational structure, key policies, and how to find help. New hires are assigned to the next cohort that fits their start date. Remote employees attend virtually unless told otherwise.

Managers must treat orientation as protected time and should not schedule conflicting mandatory meetings during the cohort session. If a new hire misses orientation due to approved absence, People Operations will assign the employee to the next every-other-Monday cohort at 9:00 AM Pacific. Orientation attendance is tracked for compliance reporting.

## Role-Based Learning and Access

Beyond the required first-week items, managers should provide role-based learning such as codebase walkthroughs, customer context, or internal operations training. Access to Confidential and Restricted systems must follow data and security policies. Managers should request only the access needed for the new hire's current responsibilities and should review access again after the first month.

Employees in customer-facing or production-support roles may have additional onboarding checklists owned by their department. Department checklists supplement, and do not replace, the company requirements in this policy, including Day-1 equipment issuance, the welcome meeting before 11:00 AM Pacific, security awareness training due by Day 5, buddy assignment by Day 2, benefits enrollment within thirty calendar days, and documented 30-day and 90-day check-ins.

## Probation Performance Expectations

During the ninety calendar day probation period, new hires are expected to demonstrate reliable attendance, respectful conduct, timely completion of required onboarding tasks, and progress against role expectations set by the manager. Meridian Software Inc. recognizes that learning curves vary by role; managers should define what "good progress" looks like by the 30-day check-in.

If employment will be ended during probation for reasons other than misconduct, Meridian Software Inc. provides one week notice or pay in lieu, as stated above. People Operations ensures that final pay, benefits ending notices, and system access removal are coordinated with IT and Payroll. Managers must not make informal separation agreements that conflict with People Operations guidance.

## Ending Employment During or After Onboarding

If employment ends for any reason, IT equipment return is due within five (5) business days of the last day. This includes the laptop (MacBook Pro or Windows laptop), employee badge, headset, and any other issued accessories. Employees must also cease using company accounts, return confidential materials, and follow People Operations instructions for exit paperwork.

Failure to return equipment within five business days of the last day may result in follow-up by IT and People Operations, and may include further action consistent with company policy and applicable law. Managers should remind departing employees of the return deadline during the exit conversation and confirm shipping labels or drop-off instructions for remote employees.

## Manager Checklist Summary

Managers supporting a Meridian Software Inc. new hire should confirm that:

1. A 30-minute Day-1 welcome meeting is scheduled before 11:00 AM Pacific.  
2. Day-1 equipment and accounts are ready with IT (laptop per role, badge, headset, email, Slack, HRIS, source control as applicable).  
3. First-week plans include security awareness training due by Day 5, benefits enrollment session, product overview, and buddy introduction.  
4. An onboarding buddy is assigned by Day 2.  
5. The new hire is registered for the new hire orientation cohort held every other Monday at 9:00 AM Pacific.  
6. Benefits enrollment progress is monitored against the thirty calendar day deadline.  
7. 30-day and 90-day check-ins occur and are documented in the HRIS.  
8. Probation expectations are clear, including that separation during probation generally requires one week notice or pay in lieu except for misconduct.

## IT Checklist Summary

IT should confirm that equipment issuance and account provisioning occur on Day 1 whenever possible, that security awareness training assignments are active immediately, and that access is appropriate for the role. IT partners with People Operations on offboarding so that equipment return within five business days of the last day and timely account deactivation both occur. IT escalates repeated delays in laptop availability to leadership when onboarding capacity is at risk.

## People Operations Coordination

People Operations monitors onboarding completion metrics, including welcome meeting compliance, buddy assignment by Day 2, security training completion by Day 5, benefits enrollment by day thirty, orientation attendance at the every-other-Monday 9:00 AM Pacific cohort, and HRIS documentation of 30-day and 90-day check-ins. People Operations may send reminders to managers and new hires when milestones are approaching or overdue.

Exceptions to onboarding timing requirements require People Operations approval, and for equipment or account exceptions, IT approval as well. Exceptions should be rare, documented, and time-bounded.

## Remote and Hybrid Onboarding

Remote and hybrid employees follow the same substantive requirements as office-based employees. The Day-1 welcome meeting must still occur before 11:00 AM Pacific, even if the employee works in another timezone, unless People Operations approves an exception for a role that is entirely based outside Pacific Time operations and cannot reasonably meet that clock time. Equipment shipping should be arranged so that the laptop, headset, and badge arrive in time for Day 1 whenever feasible. If shipment delays occur, IT documents the exception and provides interim access guidance.

Buddy introductions and orientation cohort attendance may be virtual. Managers should be intentional about inclusion so that remote new hires receive equivalent context about Meridian Software Inc. culture and tools.

## Accessibility and Accommodation During Onboarding

New hires who need disability accommodation to complete onboarding activities should contact People Operations at people@meridian-software.example as early as possible. Meridian Software Inc. will engage in an interactive process regarding reasonable accommodations for training formats, equipment, or schedules. Accommodation discussions are separate from performance evaluation but may adjust how onboarding milestones are completed when appropriate.

## Records and Privacy

Onboarding records, check-in notes, and equipment assignments are company records. Managers should document professionally in the HRIS and avoid storing sensitive personnel notes in unsanctioned personal tools. Access to onboarding and personnel information should be limited to those with a business need, consistent with Meridian Software Inc. data privacy requirements.

## Policy Updates and Contact

People Operations jointly with IT reviews this Onboarding Policy periodically. Material updates will be communicated through company channels using @meridian-software.example addresses and reflected in manager toolkits. For questions about probation, Day-1 requirements, buddy assignment, benefits enrollment timing, orientation, check-ins, or equipment return, contact People Operations at people@meridian-software.example and IT through the approved support process.

Meridian Software Inc. believes effective onboarding protects both the company and the new hire by creating clarity, access, and early feedback within the first ninety calendar days and beyond.
