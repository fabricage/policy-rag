# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy — Meridian Software Inc.

## Purpose

Meridian Software Inc. supports remote and hybrid work where role requirements, performance, collaboration needs, and security controls allow it. This policy defines eligibility, the hybrid default schedule, fully remote approval rules, core collaboration hours in Pacific Time, equipment and internet stipends, onsite expectations for employees near an office, manager check-in cadence, and network security requirements for remote work.

Remote work is a way of working, not an entitlement detached from business needs. Meridian Software Inc. may adjust remote arrangements when role duties, customer commitments, performance, or security risk require more onsite presence. This policy is owned by the VP of People and administered with People Operations, managers, IT, and Security.

## Eligibility

Eligibility for hybrid or remote work is limited to full-time employees who have completed the 90-day probation period and who have a performance rating of “Meets Expectations” or higher on their most recent applicable review or probationary assessment. Employees still within the 90 calendar day probation period are expected to follow the onsite or schedule guidance provided in their offer and onboarding plan, which may include more in-office time to support training and integration.

Part-time employees, interns, and employees on active performance improvement plans are not eligible for new fully remote designations under this policy unless the VP of People approves a documented exception. Eligibility does not guarantee a preferred schedule; the employee’s role must be suitable for remote work, and the manager must confirm that service levels and collaboration can be maintained.

Employees who fall below “Meets Expectations” may have remote privileges narrowed or paused until performance returns to an acceptable level. Such changes will be communicated in writing by the manager with People Operations support.

## Hybrid Default and Fully Remote Designations

The company default for eligible roles is a hybrid arrangement of up to 3 remote days per week. Managers may set team norms within that maximum to ensure coverage for customers and internal partners. Hybrid employees and managers should document the expected remote days and onsite days to reduce confusion, understanding that business events may require temporary changes.

Fully remote work requires Director approval and designation of the role as remote-eligible. Directors should consider customer coverage, data sensitivity, mentoring needs, and whether the role can be performed successfully without regular in-person presence. Fully remote approval must be recorded in the HRIS or the designated remote-work request workflow. A manager cannot grant fully remote status alone.

Even when fully remote, employees remain subject to core collaboration hours, security requirements, stipend rules, and any quarterly onsite obligations that apply based on home location distance to an office.

## Core Collaboration Hours

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM Pacific Time, Monday through Friday. During these hours, remote and hybrid employees must be available for meetings, Slack or equivalent chat responses within a reasonable time, and collaborative problem-solving, except when on approved PTO, sick leave, or a meeting-free focus block expressly agreed with the manager.

Working outside core hours is permitted when schedules require it, but core hours exist to reduce delays across time zones and to protect customer response times. Employees who cannot sustain availability during 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM Pacific Time on a regular basis must discuss accommodations or alternative arrangements with their manager and People Operations; unilateral permanent unavailability during core hours is not a remote-work option.

Managers should schedule recurring team meetings inside core hours whenever possible. Standing meetings outside core hours should be rare and justified.

## Home Office Equipment Stipend

Eligible employees approved for hybrid or remote work receive a one-time home office equipment stipend of $750. The stipend must be used within 60 days of remote or hybrid approval. Eligible purchases typically include ergonomic chairs, monitors, keyboards, webcams, docking stations, and similar equipment that enable safe and productive work. The stipend is not an unrestricted cash bonus; employees must follow the submission process published by People Operations and Finance, including receipts.

Equipment purchased with the stipend is intended for Meridian Software Inc. work. The company may require return of company-owned equipment separate from stipend purchases upon separation, according to IT asset rules. Employees who do not use the $750 stipend within 60 days of approval generally forfeit the unused stipend opportunity, subject to any legally required handling and any documented extension approved by the VP of People or Finance as applicable.

The stipend does not replace the need for an approved company laptop and security configuration. Employees must not use stipend funds to bypass IT procurement for devices that require endpoint management, full-disk encryption, or other mandatory controls under the information security policy.

## Monthly Internet Stipend

Approved hybrid and remote employees receive a monthly internet stipend of $50 to offset a portion of home internet costs associated with work. The $50 monthly stipend is not increased automatically for multiple household users or for premium gaming tiers. Employees must maintain internet service that is reliable enough to support video meetings and secure connectivity during core collaboration hours.

Finance and People Operations will communicate whether the stipend is paid automatically or requires periodic attestation. Providing false information about remote status to obtain the $50 stipend is misconduct. If an employee returns to a fully onsite arrangement, the monthly internet stipend ends on the schedule communicated by People Operations.

## Distance-Based Onsite Expectation

Employees living within 100 miles of a Meridian Software Inc. office must attend onsite at least one day per quarter for team planning. This requirement applies to hybrid employees and to fully remote employees whose home address is within 100 miles of an office, unless the VP of People approves a temporary exception for a documented reason such as medical leave coordination.

The quarterly onsite day should be used for planning, mentoring, and relationship-building rather than solely for solitary laptop work that could be done remotely. Managers are responsible for scheduling meaningful onsite time and for tracking attendance at a high level. Employees who repeatedly fail to meet the one-day-per-quarter expectation without approval may have remote arrangements revised.

Mileage or travel expenses for ordinary quarterly onsite attendance by employees who chose to live within 100 miles of an office are not automatically reimbursable as business travel unless Finance publishes a specific exception; employees should consult the expense policy for rules that apply to approved business travel beyond routine onsite expectations.

## Manager Check-Ins

Managers must hold check-ins at least biweekly with employees while those employees are working in a remote or hybrid arrangement. Biweekly check-ins should cover priorities, capacity, well-being signals, collaboration barriers, and performance expectations. Check-ins may be video-based and should not be replaced indefinitely by asynchronous chat alone.

Managers who supervise fully remote employees should be especially deliberate about coaching, inclusion in promotions discussions, and equitable access to high-visibility work. At least biweekly cadence is a minimum; more frequent contact may be required for new projects or performance concerns.

Employees share responsibility for surfacing blockers early and for being prepared for check-ins. Missing check-ins repeatedly without cause may be addressed as a performance or communication issue.

## Workspace and Safety

Remote employees must maintain a workspace that is reasonably safe and free from hazards that could cause injury during work time. Employees should use ergonomic setups funded in part by the $750 stipend where needed, take breaks, and report work-related injuries according to People Operations procedures.

Remote work locations must be conducive to protecting Confidential and Restricted information from eavesdropping and shoulder surfing. Employees should avoid conducting sensitive calls on speakerphone in public spaces. Family members and roommates must not use company devices.

Meridian Software Inc. may require photos of the work setup for safety coaching or may require temporary onsite work if the remote environment is unsuitable. The company does not require employees to purchase premises upgrades beyond ordinary home-office needs covered by the stipend program.

## Security and Network Requirements

Secure Wi-Fi is required for remote work. Employees must use a password-protected home network and must follow IT standards for router hygiene where published. Public café Wi-Fi is prohibited for Restricted data work without the company VPN. Employees who must work briefly from an untrusted network must connect through the company VPN before accessing systems that may process Restricted data, and should postpone Restricted work if VPN is unavailable.

Remote employees remain fully subject to the information security policy, including password length and rotation rules, MFA for email, VPN, source control, and cloud admin consoles, full-disk encryption with BitLocker or FileVault, screen lock after 5 minutes of inactivity, and incident reporting timelines. Lost devices must still be reported within 1 hour to security@meridian-software.example and IT Service Desk.

USB storage of Confidential or Restricted data remains prohibited without Security Team exception, whether the employee is at home or onsite. Security awareness training due within 30 days of hire and every 12 months thereafter remains mandatory for remote employees.

## Scheduling, PTO, and Availability

Remote work does not alter PTO accrual or request rules. Employees must still obtain manager approval for PTO, submit requests for three or more consecutive business days at least 10 business days in advance, and follow blackout rules where applicable. Working while claiming PTO is discouraged and may require correction of time records.

Employees should keep their calendars accurate, display working locations if the company tool supports it, and update status when they step away during core hours. Silence during 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM Pacific Time without notice undermines trust in hybrid arrangements.

Overtime-eligible employees must record all hours accurately and must not work “off the clock” from home. Managers must not expect overtime-eligible remote employees to be continuously available outside scheduled hours without proper time recording and approval practices.

## Equipment, IT Support, and Stipend Coordination

Company-issued laptops remain the primary computing devices for work. The $750 one-time stipend complements, but does not replace, IT-issued equipment. The $50 monthly internet stipend complements home connectivity and does not imply that IT will support all personal networking hardware indefinitely.

IT Service Desk support for remote employees is provided during published support hours. Employees should maintain a backup plan for brief outages, such as temporary phone hotspot use for non-Restricted communications when appropriate, while remembering that Restricted data work on public café Wi-Fi without VPN is prohibited.

Employees who relocate must update their address in the HRIS before the move when possible. A move that places the employee within 100 miles of an office triggers the quarterly onsite planning day requirement. A move that changes tax jurisdiction or work eligibility must be approved in advance; unauthorized international remote work is prohibited.

## Performance, Inclusion, and Revocation

Performance standards for remote and hybrid employees are the same as for onsite employees. Communication quality, responsiveness during core collaboration hours, and reliable participation in team rituals are part of how performance is evaluated. Isolation is a risk in remote work; managers should invite remote employees into discussions and decisions deliberately.

Meridian Software Inc. may revoke or modify hybrid or remote arrangements for business reasons, including role changes, customer requirements, security incidents, misuse of stipends, failure to attend required quarterly onsite days when living within 100 miles of an office, failure to maintain biweekly check-ins, or performance below “Meets Expectations.” Revocations will be communicated with a transition timeline appropriate to the circumstances.

## Policy Ownership

The VP of People owns this policy. People Operations implements workflows for eligibility verification after the 90-day probation period, Director approvals for fully remote designations, stipend tracking for the $750 one-time equipment stipend (60-day use window) and the $50 monthly internet stipend, and coaching for managers on biweekly check-ins. IT and Security own technical controls that apply to remote endpoints and VPN use. Finance owns payment mechanics for stipends and related expense processes.

Employees with questions should contact People Operations at people@meridian-software.example. Directors considering fully remote role designations should consult People Operations before making offers that depend on remote eligibility.

## Summary of Testable Requirements

Eligible employees are full-time employees who completed the 90-day probation period with a performance rating of “Meets Expectations” or higher. Hybrid work allows up to 3 remote days per week. Fully remote work requires Director approval and remote-eligible role designation. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM Pacific Time, Monday–Friday. The one-time home office equipment stipend is $750 and must be used within 60 days of remote or hybrid approval. The monthly internet stipend is $50. Employees living within 100 miles of an office must attend onsite at least one day per quarter for team planning. Managers must conduct check-ins at least biweekly while employees are remote or hybrid. Secure Wi-Fi is required, and public café Wi-Fi is prohibited for Restricted data work without the company VPN. Policy owner: VP of People, Meridian Software Inc.

Meridian Software Inc. will review this policy periodically to ensure it continues to support productive collaboration, equitable treatment of remote and onsite teammates, and strong security practices across distributed work environments.
