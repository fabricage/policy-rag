# Meridian Software Inc. Holiday Schedule Policy

## Purpose and Scope

This Holiday Schedule Policy establishes how Meridian Software Inc. provides paid time off for company-recognized holidays and floating holidays. The policy applies to all employees of Meridian Software Inc., including full-time and part-time employees, unless a written employment agreement, collective bargaining agreement, or applicable law provides a different arrangement. Contractors, temporary agency workers, and interns are covered only to the extent expressly stated in their engagement terms.

Meridian Software Inc. operates with headquarters aligned to Pacific Time for scheduling, observance rules, and deadline calculations under this policy. Questions about eligibility, scheduling, or pay treatment should be directed to People Operations at people@meridian-software.example.

## Policy Owner

People Operations owns this Holiday Schedule Policy, including interpretation, updates, and exception handling. Managers are responsible for applying the policy consistently within their teams and for approving floating holiday requests in accordance with the timelines set out below. Payroll and People Operations coordinate holiday pay calculations and corrections.

## Company Holidays Recognized

Meridian Software Inc. recognizes eleven (11) paid company holidays each calendar year. Employees should plan work, customer commitments, and personal travel around the following fixed holidays, which are listed in full for clarity and auditability:

1. New Year's Day  
2. Martin Luther King Jr. Day  
3. Presidents' Day  
4. Memorial Day  
5. Juneteenth  
6. Independence Day  
7. Labor Day  
8. Thanksgiving Day  
9. Day After Thanksgiving  
10. Christmas Eve  
11. Christmas Day  

These eleven holidays are the complete set of paid company holidays. No additional fixed company holidays are granted under this policy unless People Operations publishes a written amendment for a specific calendar year. When Meridian Software Inc. publishes an annual holiday calendar, it will map these named holidays to the applicable calendar dates and any adjusted observance days described in the weekend observance section of this policy.

## Weekend Observance Rules

If a fixed company holiday falls on a Saturday, Meridian Software Inc. observes the holiday on the preceding Friday. If a fixed company holiday falls on a Sunday, Meridian Software Inc. observes the holiday on the following Monday. These observance rules apply to the eleven paid company holidays listed above and are used for timekeeping, paid time off, and office closure decisions.

When Christmas Eve and Christmas Day fall across a weekend boundary, People Operations will publish the specific observance days for that year so that employees and managers have a single authoritative schedule. Observance announcements will be communicated through the HRIS and company email from an @meridian-software.example address. Until an announcement is published, employees should assume the Saturday-to-Friday and Sunday-to-Monday rules apply independently to each holiday date.

## Floating Holidays

In addition to the eleven fixed company holidays, Meridian Software Inc. provides two (2) floating holidays per calendar year to eligible employees. Floating holidays are intended to support cultural, religious, personal, or other meaningful observances that are not covered by the fixed holiday list.

Floating holidays must be used by December 31 of the calendar year in which they are granted. There is no carryover of unused floating holidays into the following calendar year. Floating holidays that remain unused as of December 31 are forfeited and are not paid out, except where applicable law requires otherwise. Employees who start mid-year receive a pro-rated floating holiday allotment as determined by People Operations and recorded in the HRIS; any pro-rated floating holidays remain subject to the December 31 use-or-forfeit rule.

## Requesting Floating Holidays

Employees must submit floating holiday requests at least five (5) business days in advance via the HRIS. Requests submitted with fewer than five business days of notice may be denied for operational reasons, even if the employee still has floating holiday balance available. Managers should respond to timely floating holiday requests promptly so that team coverage can be arranged.

A floating holiday request is not approved until the manager approves it in the HRIS. Employees should not assume approval based on informal conversation. If a conflict arises between multiple team members requesting the same date, managers should consider business needs, customer commitments, and fairness across the team, and may consult People Operations for guidance.

## Eligibility for Holiday Pay

Full-time employees who are actively employed and not on unpaid leave on the observed holiday date are eligible for paid company holiday pay for the eleven fixed holidays, subject to the leave rules below. Floating holiday pay is available only when a floating holiday has been approved and taken in accordance with this policy.

Part-time employees receive holiday pay only if the holiday falls on a normally scheduled workday for that employee. If a fixed company holiday, or its observed day under the weekend rules, falls on a day the part-time employee is not normally scheduled to work, the part-time employee does not receive holiday pay for that day. Part-time employees may still request floating holidays, subject to manager approval and the five-business-day advance notice requirement, if floating holiday eligibility has been granted in their offer or HRIS profile.

Employees on unpaid leave during a holiday are not paid for that holiday. This includes unpaid personal leave, unpaid leave of absence segments, and any other unpaid status that covers the observed holiday date. Employees on approved paid leave that runs through a company holiday should consult People Operations for how holiday pay interacts with the leave type; in no case does this policy create double payment for the same day beyond what Payroll and People Operations determine is consistent with company practice and law.

## Religious and Cultural Accommodation

Meridian Software Inc. respects employees' religious and cultural observances. When an observance is not covered by the eleven fixed company holidays or by an employee's remaining floating holidays, additional unpaid time or PTO may be used with Manager approval. Employees seeking religious accommodation should raise the request with their Manager as early as practicable and may involve People Operations for guidance.

Managers evaluating accommodation requests should focus on operational feasibility and consistency, and should not inquire into the sincerity of religious belief beyond what is needed to understand scheduling needs. Where unpaid time is approved, timekeeping must accurately reflect unpaid status. Where PTO is used, standard PTO balances and request procedures apply. Floating holidays, when available, remain a preferred first option for non-fixed observances because they are paid and designed for this purpose.

## Scheduling, Coverage, and Customer Commitments

Although Meridian Software Inc. recognizes the holidays listed in this policy, certain roles may be required to provide on-call coverage, incident response, or customer support on or around holidays. When business needs require work on an observed company holiday, the employee's manager and People Operations will confirm the arrangement in writing before the holiday whenever possible. Compensation for required holiday work, if any, will follow applicable wage-and-hour rules and company pay practices administered by Payroll.

Employees should not schedule customer-facing commitments on company holidays without manager approval and a clear coverage plan. Teams that support global customers should document handoffs with colleagues in other regions, remembering that Meridian Software Inc. HQ scheduling and holiday observance are based on Pacific Time even when customers operate in other time zones.

## Interaction with Probation and New Hires

New employees are eligible for paid company holidays that occur on or after their start date if they otherwise meet the eligibility rules in this policy. During the probation period of ninety (90) calendar days from the start date, holiday pay for fixed company holidays continues to apply when eligibility criteria are met; probation status alone does not eliminate holiday pay for an observed company holiday. Floating holiday eligibility for new hires is recorded in the HRIS and remains subject to the December 31 forfeiture rule and the five-business-day advance request requirement.

If employment ends before a floating holiday is used, unused floating holidays are not paid out unless required by law. Fixed holidays occurring after an employee's last day are not paid.

## Timekeeping and Payroll Treatment

Employees must record holiday time in accordance with Meridian Software Inc. timekeeping procedures. Salaried exempt employees generally receive holiday pay as part of regular salary continuation for observed company holidays, subject to leave and eligibility rules. Non-exempt employees receive holiday pay according to scheduled hours and Payroll guidance for the observed holiday, and must not work on a company holiday unless directed and approved.

Incorrect holiday coding can delay pay corrections. Employees who believe holiday pay was omitted or misapplied should contact their manager and People Operations at people@meridian-software.example promptly after the affected pay period. Corrections are processed through Payroll once People Operations confirms eligibility.

## Office Closure and Remote Work

On observed company holidays, Meridian Software Inc. offices are closed unless People Operations or Facilities announces a limited exception. Remote employees observe the same company holidays and floating holiday rules as office-based employees. Working remotely on a company holiday does not convert the day into a regular workday for pay purposes unless the employee has been expressly required to work and the arrangement is documented for Payroll.

Employees who choose to work voluntarily on a company holiday without approval may create compliance and fairness issues. Voluntary unapproved work on holidays is discouraged and may not be compensated beyond legal requirements. Managers should reinforce that rest on company holidays is the default expectation.

## Planning Guidance for Managers

Managers should review the eleven fixed holidays and team floating holiday plans at the start of each quarter. For Thanksgiving Day, Day After Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, and Christmas Day, managers should confirm coverage plans at least two weeks in advance when the team supports production systems. Managers must not unofficially substitute different holidays for the company list; substitutions require People Operations approval.

When approving floating holidays, managers should verify that the employee submitted the request at least five business days in advance via the HRIS and that the employee has floating holiday balance remaining. Managers should also confirm that approving the request will not leave critical roles without coverage. If a request must be denied, the manager should explain the business reason and help the employee identify alternative dates before December 31 to avoid forfeiture.

## Communications and Annual Calendar

Each year, People Operations publishes an annual holiday calendar that lists the dates of New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, Day After Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, and Christmas Day, including any Friday or Monday observance adjustments. The calendar also reminds employees that two floating holidays are available per calendar year, must be requested at least five business days in advance via the HRIS, must be used by December 31, and do not carry over.

Employees should rely on the published calendar and this policy rather than third-party holiday lists. If a local, state, or national public holiday is not one of the eleven company holidays and is not taken as a floating holiday, PTO, or approved unpaid accommodation, it is a regular workday for Meridian Software Inc. employees.

## Exceptions and Special Circumstances

Limited exceptions to floating holiday timing or notice requirements may be granted by People Operations in extraordinary circumstances, such as bereavement-related scheduling conflicts or sudden religious observance needs that could not reasonably be anticipated five business days in advance. Exceptions are discretionary, documented in the HRIS when feasible, and do not create a precedent for other employees.

This policy does not limit rights under applicable law. If a provision of this policy conflicts with a non-waivable legal requirement in a jurisdiction where Meridian Software Inc. employs people, the legal requirement controls for employees in that jurisdiction, and People Operations will provide localized guidance.

## Related Expectations During Holidays

Employees remain bound by Meridian Software Inc. conduct, confidentiality, and security expectations on holidays and floating holidays. Company devices and accounts should not be used for unauthorized purposes during time off. Employees who are not on approved on-call duty are not expected to monitor Slack or email on company holidays, and managers should avoid creating pressure to respond unless a genuine urgency exists.

Customer communications that reference Meridian Software Inc. holiday closures should be accurate and should list only the recognized company holidays or state that the company is closed for a recognized holiday. Misstating the holiday list can create customer confusion and internal inconsistency.

## Administration, Updates, and Contact

People Operations reviews this Holiday Schedule Policy periodically and may update it to reflect operational needs, legal changes, or company structure updates. Material changes will be communicated through company email from an @meridian-software.example address and posted where employees access policies.

For questions about holiday eligibility, floating holiday balances, weekend observance, part-time holiday pay rules, unpaid leave treatment, or religious accommodation options, contact People Operations at people@meridian-software.example. Managers who need help resolving competing time-off requests around Thanksgiving Day, Day After Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, or Christmas Day should escalate early so that coverage decisions can be made without last-minute denials that risk floating holiday forfeiture on December 31.

## Summary of Key Requirements

Employees of Meridian Software Inc. receive eleven paid company holidays: New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, Day After Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, and Christmas Day. Two floating holidays are granted per calendar year, must be requested at least five business days in advance via the HRIS, must be used by December 31, and do not carry over. Saturday holidays are observed on Friday; Sunday holidays are observed on Monday. Employees on unpaid leave during a holiday are not paid for that holiday. Part-time employees receive holiday pay only if the holiday falls on a normally scheduled workday. Additional unpaid time or PTO may be used for religious accommodation with Manager approval. People Operations owns this policy, and HQ timing references use Pacific Time.
