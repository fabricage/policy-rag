# Meridian Software Inc. Code of Conduct

## Purpose and Commitment

This Code of Conduct sets the standards of professional behavior expected of everyone who works for or represents Meridian Software Inc. The company is committed to a workplace that is respectful, ethical, inclusive, and safe. This Code applies to employees, officers, and, where applicable, contractors and temporary workers when they are performing work for Meridian Software Inc. or representing the company.

Meridian Software Inc. headquarters scheduling and response timelines under this policy are understood in Pacific Time unless a different local legal deadline applies. Questions about this Code may be directed to people@meridian-software.example or to the VP of People.

## Policy Owner

The VP of People owns this Code of Conduct, including interpretation, training expectations, and updates. People Operations administers reporting intake, investigation coordination, and disciplinary process support. Managers are responsible for modeling the Code, addressing concerns promptly, and escalating issues they cannot resolve appropriately.

## Expected Workplace Behavior

Employees of Meridian Software Inc. are expected to treat colleagues, customers, partners, and vendors with professionalism and respect. Expected behavior includes honest communication, reliable follow-through on commitments, constructive collaboration, and compliance with company policies and applicable law. Employees should raise concerns early, seek clarification when unsure, and avoid conduct that undermines trust or psychological safety.

Meridian Software Inc. values candid debate about products and strategy, but disagreement must remain respectful. Personal attacks, humiliation, or exclusionary behavior are inconsistent with this Code. Leaders have a heightened responsibility to set tone, intervene when they observe problematic conduct, and ensure that team members understand how to report concerns.

## Equal Opportunity and Respectful Workplace

Meridian Software Inc. prohibits discrimination and harassment based on protected characteristics recognized by applicable law, including but not limited to race, color, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, national origin, age, disability, and veteran status. Harassment includes unwelcome conduct that creates an intimidating, hostile, or offensive work environment, whether in person, online, or through company systems.

Harassment, discrimination, and violence are subject to zero tolerance and may result in immediate termination. This zero-tolerance standard applies to physical violence, threats of violence, sexual harassment, discriminatory harassment, and other severe misconduct. Meridian Software Inc. will take prompt action when it becomes aware of such conduct, including interim measures when appropriate to protect involved parties during an investigation.

## Reporting Channels

Employees and other covered persons who become aware of actual or suspected violations of this Code, or of related policies, should report promptly through one or more of the following channels:

- Email People Operations at people@meridian-software.example  
- Call the anonymous Ethics Hotline at 1-800-555-0199  
- Report to their direct manager  
- Report to the VP of People  

Employees may choose the channel that best fits the situation. Anonymous reports through the Ethics Hotline at 1-800-555-0199 are accepted; however, the company's ability to investigate may be limited if insufficient detail is provided. Reports involving a direct manager may be made to People Operations, the Ethics Hotline, or the VP of People instead of the manager.

## Non-Retaliation

Retaliation against good-faith reporters is prohibited and itself a conduct violation. Meridian Software Inc. will not tolerate adverse action taken because someone reported a concern in good faith, participated in an investigation, or refused to engage in conduct they reasonably believed was unlawful or against this Code. Retaliation may result in discipline up to and including termination, regardless of the outcome of the underlying report.

Good faith means the reporter provides information they believe to be true. Knowingly false reports made in bad faith are also a conduct violation and may result in discipline. A report that is not ultimately substantiated is not automatically a bad-faith report.

## Investigations

People Operations acknowledges reports within two (2) business days and aims to complete investigations within ten (10) business days. Acknowledgment confirms that the report was received and that intake has begun; it does not mean a final determination has been made. Complex matters may require more than ten business days; when that occurs, People Operations will document the reason for the extension and provide status updates to appropriate parties as confidentiality allows.

Investigations are conducted as impartially and confidentially as practical. Employees are expected to cooperate truthfully. Interfering with an investigation, destroying relevant evidence, or coaching others to provide false information is a serious violation of this Code. Interim actions during an investigation may include schedule changes, temporary reporting-line adjustments, or paid administrative leave when needed to protect safety or investigation integrity.

## Progressive Discipline

When Meridian Software Inc. determines that corrective action is appropriate for conduct that does not require immediate separation, the company generally follows progressive discipline in this order:

1. Verbal warning  
2. Written warning  
3. Final written warning  
4. Termination  

Serious misconduct may skip steps. Examples of situations that may justify skipping steps include harassment, discrimination, violence, theft, fraud, severe conflicts of interest, willful data misuse, or other conduct that creates significant risk to people, customers, or the company. Progressive discipline is a framework, not a guarantee of any particular sequence in every case. Employment relationships remain subject to applicable law and any written agreements.

Documentation of warnings and outcomes is maintained by People Operations. Managers must not issue informal "off-the-books" discipline in place of the process above when a formal conduct issue exists. Coaching for performance skill gaps may occur alongside, but should not replace, conduct discipline when the issue is behavioral or ethical.

## Conflicts of Interest

Employees must avoid conflicts of interest and situations that create the appearance of a conflict between personal interests and Meridian Software Inc. duties. Conflicts of interest must be disclosed to People Operations within five (5) business days of arising. Disclosure should include enough detail for People Operations to evaluate the conflict, recommend safeguards, or require recusal.

Examples include significant financial interests in a competitor, vendor, or customer; outside employment that interferes with Meridian Software Inc. responsibilities; supervising a close family member; or steering company business to a personal associate. Having a potential conflict is not automatically a violation; failing to disclose within five business days can be a violation. People Operations, often consulting the VP of People and relevant business leaders, determines appropriate mitigation.

## Gifts, Entertainment, and Vendor Relationships

Employees must not accept gifts or entertainment that could influence, or appear to influence, business decisions. Gifts from vendors may not exceed fifty dollars ($50) fair market value per year per vendor without VP of Finance approval. This $50 annual aggregate limit per vendor applies to tangible gifts, gift cards, and similar items of value. Meals and entertainment of modest value that are directly related to legitimate business discussions may be acceptable when consistent with company expense and anti-bribery expectations, but employees should seek guidance when unsure.

Employees may never accept cash or cash equivalents beyond de minimis promotional items of trivial value, and may never solicit gifts from vendors. If a vendor offers a gift that would exceed the $50 fair market value per year per vendor limit, the employee must decline or obtain written VP of Finance approval before accepting. Improper gift acceptance may result in discipline under the progressive discipline framework or immediate termination for serious cases.

## Confidentiality and Social Media

Employees must protect Meridian Software Inc. information and customer information entrusted to the company. On social media, employees must not disclose Confidential or Restricted company information. This includes non-public product roadmaps, unpublished financial results, customer lists, security details, personnel information, and any data classified as Confidential or Restricted under company data policies.

Personal social media activity should not claim to speak for Meridian Software Inc. unless authorized. Employees who identify their affiliation with Meridian Software Inc. online should communicate respectfully and avoid statements that constitute harassment, discrimination, threats, or unauthorized disclosure. Managers should not require employees to use personal social accounts for company marketing.

## Use of Company Systems and Resources

Company systems, devices, accounts, and workspaces are provided for legitimate business purposes. Limited personal use may be acceptable if it does not interfere with work, violate policy, or create security risk. Employees must not use Meridian Software Inc. systems to access or distribute unlawful content, to harass others, or to operate a competing business.

Employees should assume that company systems may be monitored in accordance with applicable law and company IT policies. Attempts to bypass security controls, share credentials, or access systems without authorization are serious misconduct and may skip progressive discipline steps.

## Alcohol, Drugs, and Safety

Employees must not work while impaired by alcohol or drugs, and must not possess illegal drugs on company premises or while performing company business. Alcohol at approved company events must be consumed responsibly, and employees remain accountable for their conduct. Violence, weapons where prohibited by law or company rules, and intimidation are forbidden.

Safety concerns, including threats or stalking related to the workplace, should be reported immediately through the reporting channels listed in this Code, including people@meridian-software.example, the anonymous Ethics Hotline at 1-800-555-0199, a direct manager, or the VP of People. Emergency situations should also be reported to local emergency services as appropriate.

## Customer and Partner Interactions

Employees representing Meridian Software Inc. with customers and partners must communicate honestly, honor commitments or escalate when commitments cannot be met, and avoid misrepresentation of product capabilities. Bribery and improper kickbacks are prohibited. If a customer or partner requests something that would violate law or this Code, employees should decline and escalate to their manager and People Operations or Legal as appropriate.

Feedback from customers about employee conduct should be routed to managers and, when the feedback alleges harassment, discrimination, fraud, or similar serious issues, to people@meridian-software.example or the VP of People. The same investigation acknowledgment target of two business days and completion aim of ten business days applies once People Operations opens a formal report.

## Manager Responsibilities

Managers at Meridian Software Inc. must reinforce this Code in hiring, onboarding, and ongoing team practices. Managers must take reports seriously, avoid promising absolute confidentiality beyond what the process allows, and promptly escalate allegations of harassment, discrimination, violence, fraud, or retaliation. Managers must not investigate serious allegations alone when People Operations should lead.

Managers also play a key role in preventing retaliation after a report. Checking in on team climate, ensuring work assignments remain fair, and escalating any suspected retaliatory behavior are manager duties. Failure by a manager to escalate known serious misconduct can itself be a conduct issue.

## Relationship to Employment Status

This Code applies throughout employment, including during the probation period of ninety (90) calendar days from the start date. Probationary status does not reduce expectations for ethical conduct. Serious misconduct during probation may result in immediate termination without progression through all disciplinary steps.

Nothing in this Code alters at-will employment where it applies, or creates an employment contract for a definite term. Meridian Software Inc. may update this Code and will communicate material changes through company channels using @meridian-software.example addresses where appropriate.

## Training and Acknowledgment

Employees may be required to complete Code of Conduct training and acknowledgment periodically. Completion expectations will be communicated by People Operations. Failure to complete required training without approval may result in follow-up under performance or conduct processes. Training is a support tool; it does not replace personal responsibility to follow this Code.

## Asking Questions and Seeking Guidance

Employees who are unsure whether a situation violates this Code should ask before acting. Guidance is available from direct managers, People Operations at people@meridian-software.example, and the VP of People. For sensitive concerns where anonymity is preferred, the Ethics Hotline at 1-800-555-0199 remains available.

When evaluating a gray area, employees should consider whether the conduct would withstand scrutiny by customers, regulators, and colleagues; whether it creates an unfair advantage or hidden conflict; and whether it aligns with Meridian Software Inc. values of respect and integrity. If the answer is uncertain, disclosure and guidance are the correct next steps, especially for conflicts of interest that must be disclosed within five business days of arising and for vendor gifts that may approach the $50 fair market value per year per vendor threshold.

## Enforcement and Outcomes

Substantiated violations of this Code may result in progressive discipline—verbal warning, written warning, final written warning, or termination—or immediate termination for serious misconduct, including harassment, discrimination, and violence under the zero-tolerance standard. Outcomes are determined based on severity, intent, impact, prior history, and any mitigating factors. People Operations maintains records of investigations and outcomes as required for legal and administrative purposes.

Employees who disagree with a disciplinary outcome may ask People Operations about available internal review options. Internal review does not pause an employment decision unless Meridian Software Inc. expressly agrees in writing.

## Contact and Administration

The VP of People owns this Code and may issue clarifying guidance. Day-to-day administration is handled by People Operations. Reports may be submitted to people@meridian-software.example, the anonymous Ethics Hotline at 1-800-555-0199, a direct manager, or the VP of People. People Operations acknowledges reports within two business days and aims to complete investigations within ten business days. Retaliation against good-faith reporters is prohibited and itself a conduct violation.

Meridian Software Inc. expects every person covered by this Code to help maintain a workplace worthy of our colleagues and customers. Ethical culture is built through daily choices, timely disclosures, and courage to speak up when something is wrong.
